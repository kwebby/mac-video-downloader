# Helping with MVD

Thanks for taking a look. Bug reports, clear reproduction steps, and suggestions are welcome.

Before opening an issue, check [the tutorials](Documentation/tutorials.md) and [the getting started guide](Documentation/getting-started.md). Include your macOS version, MVD version, and the steps that went wrong. A public sample URL helps when a website behaves differently.

Please leave passwords, cookies, access tokens, signed private URLs, and personal download history out of public issues. Use a public sample you can share instead.
